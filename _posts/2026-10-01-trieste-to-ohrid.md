In August we drove from Trieste to Lake Ohrid: Slovenia, Croatia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Montenegro, Albania and North Macedonia, mostly on small roads and mostly following water. The digital frames are from a Nikon D780; the rest are on colour and black-and-white film.

We started where Italy runs out: an evening on the Canal Grande and in Piazza Unità, and the castle at Miramare on the first roll of film.
